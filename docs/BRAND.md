# Brand and visual assets

SAMA's visual direction is an open, human startup marketplace: warm ivory, forest green, generous space, clear numbers, and plain-language actions. Charts and token jargon are secondary to understanding an offering.

## Original generated artwork

The SAMA mark and campus illustration were generated for this project with OpenAI image generation on 23 September 2026. They are creative assets, not photographs of Kirana AI's team, offices, or operations.

| Asset | Creative direction | Repository file |
|---|---|---|
| SAMA mark | Two equal interlocking architectural arches forming an open ribbon-like symbol; flat forest green; transparent background; no coin or financial seal | `apps/web/public/brand/sama-mark.png` |
| Innovation campus | Miniature tropical innovation campus, curved ivory walkways, forest-green and sage pavilions, a courtyard tree, warm morning light; matte architectural-model material | `apps/web/public/brand/sama-campus.png` |

The working palette is forest `#173F35`, warm ivory, sage, and restrained amber accents. shadcn/ui supplies accessible component primitives; contrast, keyboard use, mobile layout, and transaction feedback still require testing in the assembled application.

## Content boundaries

Kirana AI is fictional. Illustrations do not establish traction or partnerships. SAMA is a hackathon working name without completed trademark or domain clearance. Arbitrum and other third-party names identify technology integrations, not endorsement.

See the [product thesis](PRODUCT.md) for the story these assets support.
